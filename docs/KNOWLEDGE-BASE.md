## 4. Process and capability

### 4.1 Mesh expertise from weaving to coating

**Technology 4.0:** material & manufacturing simulation · digital twin

**Treatments:**
- Process allows changing mesh hardness, softness and formability
- Micro-structure annealing alters material physical properties
- Work-hardened material compensates forming tensions
- Laser cutting

**Coatings:**
- Alternative coating possibilities
- Pre- and post-coating processes
- Ability to modulate coating thickness and pattern
- Advanced material science for coating granule application
- Ability to define spec pattern

**Engineering:** prototyping · simulation assessments · cost evaluations · material recommendations · mesh design optimisation · ability to work to close tolerances

**Quality control:** 100% traceability · product standard harmonisation · process and quality control plans · custom-made testing equipment · light-box and video inspection

**Supply chain 4.0:** end-to-end real-time transparency · AI early-warning systems · optimised material flow / logistics 4.0

**Manufacturing 4.0:** manufacturing execution system (MES) · MRO 4.0 · predictive maintenance · human-machine interface · intelligent machine control · collaboration of man and machine

**Business 4.0:** predictive analytics · e-learning · IT security

### 4.2 Three pillars (Green Energy Brochure p.2)

1. **R&D / prototyping** — BVK uses Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) and rapid prototyping to analyse flow dynamics within the mesh structure.
2. **Integrated process harmonisation** — process reliability ensures product accuracy, a result of collaboration between BVK and its customers.
3. **Strong supply chain network** — a reliable and certified supply chain enables risk mitigation and robust supply networks.

### 4.3 Quality approach

Total Quality Management (TQM) guided by standardised control plans and Production Part Approval (PPA) procedure.

### 4.4 R&D and government recognition

Recognised by the Government of India through the **DSIR** programme for in-house R&D. Tests offered: advanced CFD analysis, grain-structure examination, topography assessment of air permeability, stress testing — to norms agreed between BVK and the customer. Collateral references "Joint R&D Programmes with Government of India".

## 9. Brand system

From "BVK Hydrotech — Brand Guidelines" (2026-09-18), supplied as a PDF in the media library.

### Colour

| Token | Hex | Source | Use | Contrast on white |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#000000` | Logo, "A BVK Group Company" | Body text, headings | 21:1 |
| Brand grey | `#58585A` | Logo, "BVK" | Secondary text, icons, UI chrome | 7.1:1 |
| Brand green | `#1DB053` | Logo, "HYDROTECH" | Marks, large decorative fills only | 2.84:1 — **below the accessibility floor** |
| Brand green (deep) | `#147B3A` | Darkened | Links, interactive text, focus rings | 5.35:1 |
| Text secondary | `#757577` | Tint of brand grey | Captions, helper text | 4.6:1 |
| Border (strong) | `#8E8E91` | Tint | Input borders, meaningful dividers | 3.3:1 |
| Border (subtle) | `#E4E4E5` | Tint | Decorative dividers | — |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Logo ground | Page and card background | — |
| Surface raised | `#F5F5F6` | Tint | Elevated panels, table stripes | — |

**Hard rule from the guidelines:** never use brand green `#1DB053` as a text colour, or as a solid button fill under light text. Use `#147B3A` for anything interactive that must read as on-brand green.

Everything except the three wordmark colours is flagged "Assumption" in the guidelines and still needs client sign-off.

### Print palette (posters and brochures)

The printed collateral uses a navy/blue system that is **not** in the brand guidelines: a deep navy around `#16427F`, a mid blue around `#0876C9`, serif display headings, and hexagon motifs. The Electrolyser Solutions page template on this site follows that printed blue system; the rest of the site follows the green/ink tokens in `app/globals.css`.

### Typography

No brand typeface was supplied. The guidelines use a system UI stack as an explicit placeholder; this site uses Outfit (display) and Inter (body) until a real typeface is approved.

### Spacing and radius

4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 px spacing scale; radii 4 px (inputs), 8 px (cards, buttons), 16 px (modals, large panels). Both are flagged as assumptions.

### Logo rules

- Use the lockup whole. Do not crop the "A BVK Group Company" line, recolour any part, or redraw the mark.
- Clear space on every side of at least the height of the "B" in "BVK".
- Place on white or very light neutral backgrounds only — a reversed/knockout version has not been supplied and has not been tested on dark or coloured grounds.

### Open items flagged by the guidelines themselves

Brand typeface · neutral greys, borders and surface tints · spacing and radius scale · an icon set · tone-of-voice and copy guidelines · a reversed/knockout logo · sign-off on `#147B3A` as the link/focus colour.
